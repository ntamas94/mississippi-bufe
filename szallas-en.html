About the rooms

We have six rooms with private entrances and 25 beds in total. Each room has its own bathroom, a separate toilet, a TV and internet access. Rooms sleep 1–4 people. Smoking is not allowed in the rooms.

The buffet next to the rooms takes care of meals. Breakfast is optional — 500 Ft per person on request (bread, tea, ham, butter, jam). Outside buffet hours, ring the bell at the buffet entrance for your room key.

  • 6 rooms, 1–4 guests each
  • Own bathroom and separate WC
  • Breakfast for a surcharge
  • Free parking in the yard
  • Buffet and terrace on site
  • Special rate for longer stays — just ask
