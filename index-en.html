A family place by the road

Mississippi Büfé has offered the same thing for years: quick, honest cooking and friendly service. In summer the non-smoking terrace, in winter the warm dining room. Behind the house is Motel Missouri with six rooms — for truckers, travellers passing through and guests visiting the area.
