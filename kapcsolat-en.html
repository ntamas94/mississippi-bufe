Getting here

Egyházasrádóc lies on Route 86, roughly halfway between Körmend and Szombathely. The buffet is right by the road; you can park in front of the house and in the yard — lorries included.

  • From Körmend: about 10 km north on Route 86
  • From Szombathely: about 20 km south on Route 86
  • From the Austrian border (Rábafüzes): Route 8, then Route 86
